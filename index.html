Privacy Policy

Paimana (پیمانہ) — Last updated: 22 October 2026

Paimana is a unit conversion app for property, gold, cloth and general measurements, built for use in Pakistan. This page explains what information the app uses and how.

Data we collect

Paimana does not create user accounts, and does not collect or transmit any personal information to us. We run no server that receives data from the app, so we receive nothing about you or how you use it. The app makes one network request, to look up currency exchange rates; it is described under "Third-party services" below.

Location

The app requests your device's location only when you tap the Home screen's location label or open the Find Qibla feature. It uses location on your device to show a nearby city label and to calculate the direction and distance to the Kaaba. Your precise coordinates stay on your device and are never saved or shared. You can deny this permission and use every other feature of the app normally.

Data stored on your device

Your language, dark mode, land-unit-standard preference, conversion history, location permission status, and the most recent exchange-rate table are saved only in the app's local storage on your own device, so they persist between sessions. The nearby city label and precise coordinates are not persisted. This data never leaves your device, is not backed up to any server we control, and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

Third-party services

Paimana does not use any advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

The currency converter looks up exchange rates from open.er-api.com, a free public rates service that needs no account or key. This is the only network request the app makes. It carries no account, device, or advertising identifier, and nothing about you, your location, or your conversions — it asks only for the current rate table. As with any request over the internet, the provider can see the IP address it came from; that service is not operated by us, and we receive nothing from it. Rates are cached on your device for up to 12 hours, and the app falls back to rates bundled inside the app when you are offline.

If we add any other third-party service in a future update, this policy will be updated first.

Children's privacy

Paimana does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
