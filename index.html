Privacy Policy

Paimana (پیمانہ) — Last updated: 23 September 2026

Paimana is a unit conversion app for property, gold, cloth and general measurements, built for use in Pakistan. This page explains what information the app uses and how.

Data we collect

Paimana does not create user accounts and does not collect or transmit any personal information to us or to any third party.

Location (Qibla direction)

The app requests your device's location only when you open the Find Qibla feature. This location is used solely, on your device, to calculate the direction and distance to the Kaaba. It is never sent to any server, never stored, and never shared with anyone. You can deny this permission and use every other feature of the app normally.

Data stored on your device

Your language, dark mode, land-unit-standard preference, and conversion history are saved only in the app's local storage on your own device, so they persist between sessions. This data never leaves your device, is not backed up to any server we control, and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

Third-party services

Paimana does not currently use any advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. If this changes in a future update, this policy will be updated first.

Children's privacy

Paimana does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to feedback@paimana.app.
